Who we are

Not too serious. Seriously.

Celestial Misfits is a community Rust server built by MoonDoggy, a Marine and longtime admin who'd rather you laugh at a cow flying out of a catapult than rage-quit over a raid.

Every few weeks something new is going on: a season with its own token to earn, its own shop, its own banner. Some tokens are spent and gone. One, the Eagle, Globe & Anchor, is earned in combat and kept for life.

The seasons

Each season drops its own token. Earn it in the field, spend it in that season's shop. Dates are inclusive, Central time.
