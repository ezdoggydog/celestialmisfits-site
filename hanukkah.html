Hanukkah

The Festival of Lights. Eight nights remembering the rededication of the Temple in Jerusalem, and the little jar of oil that burned for eight days. On Celestial Misfits, the moon goes gold and blue, and every night of the eight you can earn Hanukkah Gelt.

Dates

When it runs

Hanukkah follows the Hebrew calendar, so it lands on different dates every year. The season opens on the evening of the first candle and stays on for all eight nights. Dates are Central time.

What happens

When the first candle is lit, the server wishes everyone a happy Hanukkah and the Gelt starts dropping. Log in during the season and you get a reminder of what's on.

When it ends: Chag sameach, and thanks for celebrating with us.

Chanukah, Hanukkah, Hannukah? Spell it however you like. The story goes that any spelling is right, as long as it has eight letters.

Earning Gelt

Kills during the season can drop Hanukkah Gelt straight into your inventory. Bigger targets, better odds. Bradley and the patrol helicopter always pay, to whoever lands the killing blow.

TargetChanceGelt

Chickens and horses never drop Gelt. Leave the poor things alone.

Commands

Type these in game chat. Tap one to copy it.

  • /gelt shopSee what Gelt can buy
  • /gelt buy <#> [times]Buy item # from the shop (optionally several)
