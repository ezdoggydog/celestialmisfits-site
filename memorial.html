Memorial Day

Memorial Day honors those who died in service. For this week, Celestial Misfits remembers them. Earn Memorial Plaque.

Dates

What it's about

Memorial Day honors the men and women who died while serving in the United States military. This week, Celestial Misfits remembers them.

Take a moment. Then play the way they'd want you to: together, and looking out for each other.

Veterans: if it's a heavy day, you're not alone. Call or text 988, then press 1, or type /lifeline in game (private). More here.

How it works

  • Kills can drop Memorial Plaque: scientists, tunnel and underwater dwellers, and the big wild animals. A Bradley or a patrol helicopter pays the most.
  • Spend Memorial Plaque in /plaque shop.
  • Veterans: if it's a heavy day, you're not alone. Call 988, then press 1, or type /lifeline (private).

Earning Memorial Plaque

Kills during the season can drop Memorial Plaque straight into your inventory. Bradley and the patrol helicopter always pay, to whoever lands the killing blow.
