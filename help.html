SUICIDE:  "Just ask for help!"

I'll let you in on a little secret about this.
Sometimes you can't.  People have this rapid onset episode and it overwhelms them with a darkness that, unless you have experienced it, "normal" people can never comprehend.

It starts slowly, and always in a different manner.  Always.  Because they have learned to deal with the other ways that have puts them into that position.

Every single time it's different.  Every. Single. Time.

And before they know it, the demons have them locked up and they are in the midst of a  full panic attack.  They don't think rationally, they are just.. alone.  Utterly, desperately, alone.

Nothing scares them like these episodes.  The darkness is so overpowering.

What can you do?  Each person is different, but if they start acting even SLIGHTLY out of character, time to check in with them.

Just a simple "Aight?" is good.  And keep checking till they appear to be back to normal.

Because this is how some slip under the wire.  But mostly, "Hey I'll swing by and we can grab a beer!" is the best way to help.  They will resist, but you will insist.

 Because they want that help, but don't want to be weak or be a burden.

Be a pain.  It works.
