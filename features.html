How it works

What all this stuff actually does

The player guide tells you what to type. This tells you what happens when you do — including the parts that catch people out. Everything here is ours, written for this server.

Backpack Trading Cruise control Repair boxes Horses Portals Quests

The backpack

A second inventory that follows you, split into pages. It survives death and it survives wipe. Most of what people ask about is the toolbar across the top.

  • /bp open it (/backpack works too)

Pages

Each page is its own 48 slots. Click a page number to switch. You can rename a page by typing over the name beside the page buttons and pressing Enter, and you can give a page a type — Food, Components, Ammo, Weapons and so on — by clicking the type button next to the name.

Gather

Per page, and it decides what goes in automatically as you play:

  • Off — nothing goes in by itself.
  • Existing — picked-up items join stacks the page already has.
  • All — anything that fits the page's type goes in.

Search, Craft and Combine

Search finds an item anywhere in the backpack and tells you which page it's on — the page button beside the box jumps you there, and the +N beside it steps through the rest. Matching slots get a highlighted frame.

Combine merges part-stacks and tidies everything up. If the maths doesn't come out exactly right it puts the backpack back the way it was and tells you, so nothing is ever lost to it. /backpack undo reverses the last Combine if nothing's changed since.

Deposit

Sweeps your inventory — not your belt — into the backpack. It only takes things the backpack already holds, or things that match a page's type, so it won't hoover up your gun.

Trash

A bulk delete with a deliberate safety on it, because it's the one button that can cost you something.

  • Click once and it arms, showing "Sure?" for five seconds.
  • Click again while it's armed and it turns ON and goes red.
  • Leave it alone and it disarms itself.

While it's ON, anything you drag out of the backpack is deleted — the whole stack, not one item. That's the point: it's for clearing out junk without confirming every single thing. It turns itself off when you close the backpack, so it can't be left on by accident.

If you delete something you wanted: /backpack untrash brings the last one back. It remembers the last twenty — but only in memory, so a server restart clears that list. Don't rely on it overnight.

Trading

A proper trade window between two players, so nobody has to drop things on the ground and trust each other.

  • /trade <player> ask someone to trade
  • /ta accept a request
  • /td turn one down
  • /tcancel call off a request you sent

Closing the trade window also cancels it, so you don't have to remember the command.

Note the name: it's /tcancel, not /tpc — /tpc belongs to the teleport plugin and cancels a teleport instead.

Cruise control

Set a speed, let go of W, steer. The long haul around the ring road stops being a thumb exercise.

  • /cruise hold whatever speed you're doing now
  • /cruise 50 hold 50
  • /cruise auto follow the ring road too — hands off entirely
  • /cruise 40 auto both (the words go in either order)
  • /cruise off let go

It eases off by itself where the road crosses a railway, and the note on screen says CROSSING while it does. Trains do not brake for you.

It works on everything you can drive — vanilla vehicles, the custom ones, and anything added later — because it holds the forward key in your own input rather than reaching into each type of vehicle. Nothing has to be taught about it.

Repair boxes

Tag a box as a repair box, and anything in it that wears out comes back to full condition a couple of seconds later. Leave a broken hatchet in it and it's a hatchet again.

  • /repairbox look at a box and run this — it's a repair box now (run it again to stop)
  • /repairbox list your repair boxes, and where they are

Tagging a box also repairs whatever's already inside, so you don't need to take everything out and put it back.

It repairs, it doesn't resurrect. Rust drops an item's maximum condition a little every time it's repaired, and this does the same. A much-repaired tool is never quite new again — just like at a repair bench.

Horses

Claim a horse, read its genes, and breed it. Foals inherit from both parents, with a chance of something better than either.

  • /claim claim the horse you're looking at
  • /breed start breeding two horses you own
  • /claim foal turn a Foal Token into a real horse
  • /horsefind list nearby horses and point an arrow at the closest

What a horse carries

  • Speed — how fast it moves
  • Stamina — the size of its stamina bar
  • Endurance — how long it runs before tiring
  • Health — hit points
  • Generation — one higher than its parents
  • Breed — coat and type (Thoroughbreds get a speed bonus)

A foal averages its parents with a bit of variance, and can mutate — in stats or coat. That's how you end up with something neither parent was.

Breeding

Keep the pair close together and they keep producing. Each foal arrives as a Foal Token, in a green vehicle-parts crate between the two horses — so look down, not around. /claim foal turns the token into the animal.

One pair at a time, server-wide. If breeding won't start, somebody else is already at it. Ask in Discord rather than assuming it's broken.

Portals

Walk into the sphere and you come out somewhere else — another point on this map, or another server entirely.

  • /portals where the pads are

A local hop is instant. A trip to another server is a real handover: your inventory and your RP are taken off you, packaged, sent across, and given back on the other side. Nothing can be dropped or duplicated while it's in flight.

The one rule: your gear is moved, never copied, and never quietly deleted. If anything can't be placed back on you at the far end, it's archived under a six-digit code and staff can restore it. If a trip goes wrong, that code is what you bring to Discord.

One thing to do before you step in: empty your Backpacks bag. If it can't be emptied the trip is cancelled before anything is touched — which is the safe outcome, but it's easier to just do it first.

Quests

A daily board plus a pile of standing jobs, with quest givers scattered about who hand out their own work.

  • /sq open the quest list

The board

Board quests rotate every day, with the weekly ones turning over on Monday. One of each day's quests is FEATURED and pays a bonus — worth checking before you pick.

Standing jobs don't rotate. They're always there, and you can take them whenever.

Two kinds of job

  • Acquire — gather, catch, craft, loot, grow, recycle or buy it after taking the job. Things you already had don't count.
  • Hand in — turn in what you've already got. No need to take the job first.

That distinction catches everybody once. If a job isn't ticking up, check which kind it is.

Events count

Oil rigs, cargo, Bradley, heli, the train, convoy, harbour, airfield, pirates, the trucker, random raids and raidable bases all give quest credit — and for Bradley and heli, everyone who hit it gets credit, not just whoever landed the last shot.

Quest givers

Benny has the board: the daily and weekly rotation, and every standing job that isn't assigned to somebody else. Other givers hand out their own work, so it's worth talking to people you meet.
